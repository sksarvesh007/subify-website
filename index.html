Architecture
Designed for distraction-free listening.
01 / DUAL PLAYER BRIDGE

Spotify & Apple Music

Auto-detects active playback from Spotify and Apple Music with unified media control and zero configuration.

02 / REAL-TIME SYNC

Zero-Latency Sync Engine

High-precision millisecond LRC lyric parsing synchronized to real-time player position with offset controls.

03 / TRANSCRIPT DRAWER

Click-to-Seek Drawer

Open the translucent side transcript and double-click any lyric line to jump player playback to that timestamp.

04 / ZERO LATENCY CACHE

SQLite Persistent Cache

Lyrics are stored locally in ~/.subify/cache.db for instant repeat playback with zero network delay.
